# Operator source: nepalintrepidtreks.com

Scraped 2026-08-08 from `https://www.nepalintrepidtreks.com/`. Everything below is
quoted or summarised from that site. Anything I could not find there is listed
under "Still missing" rather than guessed.

**This changes the project's premise.** The design was cloned from
`annapurnacircuittrek.co.uk`, which sells for **6K Nepal Treks** (Pokhara, GBP, UK
market). The real operator for `gokyolaketrek.com` is a different company:
**Nepal Intrepid Treks & Expedition Pvt. Ltd.** (Kathmandu, USD, international
market). `content/config.ts` has been repointed. No page copy has been rewritten.

## Punctuation note

House style bans em-dashes and `pnpm lint` enforces it across `.md` too. Several
reviews below contain em-dashes in the original. Where quoted, that punctuation
has been normalised to a comma, colon or full stop. Wording is unchanged. The
originals are on TripAdvisor, linked below, if exact punctuation matters.

### Included

- All ground transport in a private vehicle, airport pick-up and drop-off
- Round-trip flight Kathmandu or Ramechhap to Lukla, including departure taxes
- Three nights in Kathmandu, 3-star hotel, bed and breakfast
- Full board on trek, breakfast, lunch and dinner, with a cup of tea
- Best available twin-share lodge-to-lodge accommodation on trek
- Attached bathroom with hot shower in Lukla, Phakding and Namche
- Experienced English-speaking guide, and a porter per two clients, including
  their food, salary, accommodation and insurance
- Seasonal fruit on trek
- TIMS and Sagarmatha National Park permits
- Nepal Intrepid Treks T-shirt, duffle bag and trekking map
- Sleeping bag and down jacket for the trek
- Trip achievement certificate
- Government taxes and office service charges

Personal Sherpa guide and oxygen cylinder with mask are available **at extra
charge**, not included.

### Not included

- Nepal entry visa fee
- International flights
- Travel insurance
- Lunch and dinner in Kathmandu
- All cold drinks, water and any beverage
- Optional excursions (culture, festival, monastery meditation, bird watching,
  farming trips)
- Personal trekking equipment such as poles
- Tips for drivers, guides and porters
- Personal expenses: phone, internet, laundry, bar bills, extra porters
- Costs from landslide, road blockage, rescue, flight cancellation or anything
  beyond their control

## Reviews that actually mention Gokyo

Four of the published reviews name Gokyo. Three distinct trips, see the duplicate
warning below. Source is TripAdvisor via their `/reviews/` page.

### 1. ChiChi, 10 March 2025, "A Dream Come True: My Trek to Gokyo Lake"

The only pure Gokyo Lake Trek review, and the strongest. Guide: **Jiban**. Notes
a special lodging request at Gokyo being arranged, and a birthday cake ordered
for her in the mountains. Says she has now done six treks with this operator.
Climbed Gokyo Ri and saw Everest and the turquoise water.

### 2. Hung Chin Chiu, 18 April 2026, "Gokyo Lake Trek in Nepal"

Taiwan. EBC and Gokyo Lakes, 28 March to 15 April 2026. Light snow on the Gokyo
section, slick descent from melting snow, no major obstacle. Makes the point the
site should be built on: "While EBC remains one of Nepal's most popular routes
during the peak seasons (March to May and September to November), far fewer
trekkers venture onto the Gokyo circuit."

### 3. yong Syuan Lin, 22 December 2025, "Most Rewarding trip of my Life to EBC and Gokyo Lake via Chola Pass"

Second trek with the operator, EBC plus Gokyo via Cho La. Trekked in November on
Lal Gurung's advice that November and December are good if you are comfortable
with cold. Praises acclimatisation pacing.

### Duplicate warning

"Melani Lin, 17 April 2026" and "Hung Chin Chiu, 18 April 2026" are the same
trip, same dates (3/28 to 4/15), same content. One is a rough machine translation
and the other a cleaned-up rewrite, published as two separate reviewers a day
apart. **Do not quote both as two people.** Ask which is the real attribution.

## Problems found on the source site

Worth telling the operator, and worth not copying:

1. The Gokyo page's meta description says "12-day itinerary". The package is
   **15 days**. That is the description Google shows.
2. The Gokyo page body says to contact `info@intrepidtreks.com`. The real address
   everywhere else is `info@nepalintrepidtreks.com`. That domain typo is on a
   money page.
3. About page says TripAdvisor "150+ reviews", the rest of the site says 156.
4. Trip cards render the rating as "5.0/ 10", which reads as five out of ten.
   It appears to mean "5.0 from 10 reviews".
5. The "twitter" social icon links to their Facebook page.
6. Homepage "Fixed Departures" section says "No data available".
7. Several blog cards render "Invalid date".
8. The Gokyo route widget and the written itinerary disagree, see above.
9. No price on the Gokyo money page, while every comparable trek has one.
