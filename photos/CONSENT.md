## Why this file still exists

The Annapurna build had to pull its hero photograph after publishing because it
showed identifiable guests whose permission had never been obtained. This file
is the record that the same thing cannot happen here.

**If a new photograph is added, it does not inherit this clearance.** Add it to
the table with its own status before it goes on a page.

## Where the people photographs are worth using

They are the site's strongest asset and the one thing a competitor cannot copy.
A stock-photo trekking site cannot show you the guide by name.

- `/about`, the team and the guiding
- `/guide-or-solo`, which argues about what a guide is for
- `/reviews`, alongside reviews that name the guides
- The homepage "why book direct with us" section

Do not caption a person with a name unless the name is confirmed. Consent to
appear is not the same as consent to be named.

## Provenance

Source JPEGs are in `fwdphotos/` and `fwdphotos (1)/` at the repo root, straight
off a phone, 3 to 5.6 MB each. Unlike the Annapurna build, where the originals
were destroyed during a folder reorganisation, these are intact. Keep them: the
WebPs in `web/` are 1350 to 2400 px on the longest edge and cannot be re-cropped
tightly or re-encoded larger.

`manifest.csv` maps every WebP back to its original filename, both sets of
dimensions, the final byte size and the quality it landed on.
